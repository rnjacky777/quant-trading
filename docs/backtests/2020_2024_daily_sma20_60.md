# Daily SMA20/60 development backtest

Run date: 2026-10-21

This run verifies the backtest pipeline. It is development evidence, not an out-of-sample result or a profitability claim.

## Configuration

- Exchange data: Binance public spot OHLCV
- Period: 2020-01-01 through 2024-12-31 UTC
- Symbols: BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT
- Timeframe: 1d
- Initial cash: 10,000 USDT per independent run
- Strategy: long/cash SMA20 above/below SMA60
- Signal: completed daily close
- Execution: next daily open
- Fee: 10 bps per side
- Adverse slippage: 5 bps per side
- Final open position: liquidated at the final close with costs

## Results

| Symbol | Strategy | Total return | CAGR | Sharpe | Sortino | Max drawdown | Trades | Exposure |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| ETH/USDT | SMA20/60 | 759.8% | 53.8% | 0.99 | 1.45 | -58.7% | 16 | 56.0% |
| ETH/USDT | Buy and hold | 2,445.7% | 91.1% | 1.20 | 1.77 | -79.3% | 1 | 99.9% |

## Interpretation

The BTC trend baseline substantially reduced maximum drawdown and improved Sharpe relative to buy and hold, while producing less absolute return. On ETH it reduced drawdown but underperformed buy and hold on return, Sharpe, and Sortino. Drawdowns of roughly 49% and 59% remain too large for live use.

These results are dominated by a strong crypto bull-market sample. They have not passed parameter sensitivity, validation, locked holdout, portfolio, or forward-paper tests. No parameter changes should be justified using future holdout results and still label those results out of sample.
